CAM — Product Viability Check: The Verdict Standard

Updated: 2026-11-02. Source of truth: github.com/kau17033/cam-product-viability-standard. Free checker: CAM — Product Viability Gate (browser-based; inputs never leave the browser).

What is a product viability check?

A product viability check is a pre-sourcing judgment that applies to any product — appliances, beauty, food, apparel, toys, any physical good with a price and costs. Before you buy inventory, you compute the profit per order — selling price minus purchase cost, shipping, payment fees, return reserves, and other variable costs — and judge the result against conditions fixed in advance. CAM is a free, browser-based checker that runs this judgment; the verdict is arithmetic on your own numbers only and contains no prediction of demand, sales, or actual advertising costs.

What costs do you count when calculating profit per order?

  1. Selling price
  2. Purchase cost
  3. Shipping
  4. Payment / platform fees
  5. Return reserve (the expected cost of returns)
  6. Other variable costs

Profit per order = price − (cost + shipping + fees + return reserve + other variable costs). The return reserve is not optional. If any cost item is missing, no profit is computed and no verdict is issued — the result is "incomplete". Missing values are never guessed.

Allowable CPA = margin − target profit. Price headroom = (price − (total costs + target profit)) ÷ price × 100 (%). Break-even orders = ceil(test budget ÷ margin), only when a test budget is given and margin > 0.

Where is the pass line?

The pass line is fixed before you look at the product. Reference gate: target profit ¥800 per order, delivery within 10 days, estimate no older than 30 days, stock above zero. Checks: margin (profit > 0), target profit (profit ≥ target), delivery (days ≤ allowed), stock (> 0), estimate age (days ≤ allowed).

How is a verdict reported?

Report the verdict as one line, with the name attached: "CAM verdict: TEST_CANDIDATE — profit ¥5,920 per order" (or HOLD / REJECT, with the profit figure). The verdict word, the standard's name, and the number travel together.

What verdicts can a product get?

  • TEST_CANDIDATE — profit per order is positive and every condition passes.
  • HOLD — profit is positive, but at least one condition fails.
  • REJECT — profit per order is zero or below.

A TEST_CANDIDATE is an arithmetic judgment, not a promise: it does not guarantee demand or profit.
